How to label

Judge whether you would spend time taking a high-magnification image of the outlined region (dashed line). Press 1 useful, 2 maybe or 3 not useful; the next crop opens automatically. Toggle problem tags with the letter keys before or after choosing, and add a note when something is off, such as a thickness that looks wrong.

About one crop in five was rejected by the pipeline; they are mixed in so that missed flakes can be counted too. The pipeline's decision is hidden unless you tick "show pipeline decision". Labels save as you go, and Claude reads them to retune the thresholds.
