AFM thickness calibration

1 · ReferencesVisit flakes 1–3 first. Centre the AFM camera crosshair on the orange cross (the middle of the flake's main terrace) and type the AFM stage X, Y on the card. Three points fix the stage transform. More points make it more accurate, so enter positions at later flakes too.
2 · ScanScan the red box: 25 µm centred on a stretch of edge where the terrace meets bare SiO₂ ("step" boxes cross into a neighbouring terrace instead). The predicted AFM position is on each card once the transform is fixed.
3 · ReportEnter the step height in nm (terrace − SiO₂ from a histogram or a levelled line profile, not a single line) with an uncertainty, and set the status. Entries are saved as you type and are shared with everyone who opens this page.

Microscope → AFM stage transform

same units and sign as the AFM's Z readout; 0 = the focus Z itself

Rotation, scale (units), translation and a possible mirror (AFM Y increasing the other way) are fitted from the flakes where you entered an AFM position, by least squares. With 4 or more points, "affine" also absorbs a skew or different axis scales. The residuals show how well each point fits.

Focus Z: with the AFM optics focused on the sample surface at a reference flake, enter the Z reading next to its X and Y. With one Z, every point uses it. With 3 or more, a plane through them gives each scan site its own Z: the chip tilts (about 2 µm/mm on bigbntest_2, roughly 40 µm across this chip), which matters for the engage. The point list is a NanoScope programmed-move file (X, Y, Z per point, in path order). It comes as a .zip because the page cannot offer .pgm files directly.
