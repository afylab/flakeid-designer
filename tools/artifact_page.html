Optics-Artifact Removal

What the 100x artifact removal does to each frame, in the high-contrast view (colour ±8 % around the terrace) where artifacts show. Switch stages on the large image to compare them in place; the difference maps show exactly what each step changed.

Stages and maps
  • Raw: dark subtracted only.
  • Flat field: divided by the 100x flat field measured on bare substrate (vignetting, dust in the light path on the day it was taken).
  • Pattern divided: then divided by the run's static optics pattern, i.e. what stays at the same pixel in every frame (dust, camera specks, soft blobs).
  • + 3-position median: the median of the frame and its two shifted frames, aligned on the flake. Anything that moves with the optics drops out; anything misaligned can leave doubled edges.
  • Difference maps: grey ±3 % of the terrace brightness, white = brighter after the step. Structure that follows the flake (its edges, terraces, debris) in a difference map means that step altered the sample, not just the optics.
  • Old median: what the export produced until today, where the shifted frames were combined ~90–175 px out of alignment. This is the source of the ghost edges; it is fixed (frames are now aligned by template matching and used only when they match).
  • Frames: the ones the median changed most, the ones the pattern changed most, and a random set. F flicks every card between raw and the final stage.
